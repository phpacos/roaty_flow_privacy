Privacy policy

Scope and commitment

This Privacy Policy applies to the Roasty Flow Chrome extension. Roasty Flow does not sell personal information, display advertising, use third-party analytics, or transmit your productivity records to the developer.

The extension handles information only to provide its user-facing productivity, focus tracking, capture, review, and membership-validation features.

Data handled

Information processed by the extension

Information you create

  • Goals, tasks, task groups, schedules, progress, and reminders.
  • Flow sessions, planned duration, status, and completion information.
  • Notes and captured assets, including titles, body text, source links, and linked tasks.
  • Preferences and interface settings.

Browsing activity during an active Flow

While you have deliberately started a Flow, Roasty Flow may process the active page's domain, URL, title, visit time, duration, visit count, and the productivity classification you assign. This information powers planned-versus-actual review, attention summaries, and the Flame score. Roasty Flow does not continuously track browsing when no Flow is active.

Local storage

This information is stored through chrome.storage.local in the Chrome profile where the extension is installed. It is not synchronized to a developer account and is not transmitted to a Roasty Flow server.

External service

How Roasty Flow interacts with Skool

Access to Roasty Flow is limited to active members of the Guruless Business Builders community. When you select Open community and validate, Chrome opens the community on Skool using your existing browser session.

The extension locally examines the resulting Skool page address and limited, transient page signals needed to distinguish the community, its public About page, and technical error pages. These validation signals are not sent to the developer or stored as community content. Roasty Flow does not read or store your Skool password or authentication cookies.

Membership confirmation is stored locally for up to 24 hours. A technical failure does not extend that period. Skool receives the ordinary network information that any website receives when you visit it and handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you explicitly use a feature that prepares a Skool draft, Roasty Flow opens Skool and places your chosen content into the editor for your review. It does not publish the post automatically.

Sharing & retention

No sale or unrelated disclosure

Roasty Flow does not sell, rent, or transfer your information to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. Data is retained locally until you delete it, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall the extension. Removing the extension normally removes its local Chrome storage from that profile.

Your choices

Control over your information

  • Do not start a Flow when you do not want browsing activity measured.
  • Edit or delete tasks, goals, and assets from within Roasty Flow.
  • Review every prepared Skool draft before deciding whether to publish it.
  • Uninstall Roasty Flow to stop all extension processing and remove its local data.

Limited Use

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

Roasty Flow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information is used only to provide or improve Roasty Flow's prominent, user-facing features. It is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or personalized advertising, and is not transferred for unrelated purposes.

Policy changes

Updates to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised date. If an update changes how the extension handles data, the Chrome Web Store disclosures and any required in-product notices will be updated as well.

Support

Questions or privacy requests

For support, privacy questions, or deletion guidance, open an issue in the Roasty Flow support repository. Do not include passwords, authentication cookies, private community content, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
